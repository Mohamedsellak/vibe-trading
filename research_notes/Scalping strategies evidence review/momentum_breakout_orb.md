# Intraday Momentum, Volatility-Expansion Breakouts, and Opening-Range / Session-Open Strategies (ES/NQ/MES/MNQ, BTC/ETH perps), evidence as of Nov 2026

Source-quality legend used below: **[PR]** peer-reviewed journal; **[WP]** academic working paper / SSRN, not peer-reviewed; **[VENDOR-WP]** SSRN paper by authors who sell related products/services (Concretum Group / Concretum Research sells strategies, data and research; Andrew Aziz runs Bear Bull Traders / Peak Capital Trading, a day-trading education/prop business), so there is a **conflict of interest**; **[REPL]** independent replication (GitHub/blog, not peer-reviewed); **[PRACT]** practitioner blog/summary; **[MKT]** marketing.

IMPORTANT FRAMING FOR THE REPORT WRITER: none of the strategies with the best evidence here are 1–5 minute "scalps". The noise-area momentum strategy enters at 10:00 ET or later and holds up to the close (hours); the ORB holds from 09:35 to a 10R target or the close; the Gao et al. signal holds only the last 30 minutes but the signal is the first 30 minutes. Evidence at genuinely minute horizons (1–15 min) points to **reversal / negative autocorrelation**, not momentum, especially in BTC. If the report recommends one of these, it should recommend an intraday-to-close holding period, not a scalping holding period.

## 1. Intraday momentum evidence (Gao et al.; Baltussen et al.; Zarattini-Aziz-Barbon noise area; crypto)

### Takeaway
Market intraday momentum (first half-hour predicts last half-hour) is real in-sample in peer-reviewed work on SPY 1993–2013 and in 60 futures markets 1974–2020, but the unconditional version has weakened or disappeared out of sample and in the 0DTE era (2022–2026). The Zarattini/Aziz/Barbon "noise area" variant (SPY, Sharpe 1.33 claimed 2007–2024) replicates in-sample in two independent GitHub studies, but both find a sharp post-publication decay (Sharpe about zero in 2025–2026). The crypto analogues (Shen/Urquhart/Wang 2022) failed an independent pre-registered out-of-sample test on Binance perps 2022–2026, with no gross edge.

### Cited Findings

**A. Gao, Han, Li & Zhou (2018 JFE), "Market intraday momentum" [PR; read working-paper version Dec 2015]**
- Data: SPY high-frequency data, 1 Feb 1993 to 31 Dec 2013; days with fewer than 500 trades excluded. — [Gao et al. working paper PDF](https://c.mql5.com/forextsd/forum/173/intraday_momentum_-_the_first_half-hour_return_predicts_the_last_half-hour_return.pdf)
- Signal definition: first half-hour return r1 is measured from the **previous day's close to 10:00 ET** (so it includes the overnight gap). Target: last half-hour return r13 (15:30–16:00). Scaled slope 6.94 (1% significance), in-sample R² 1.6%. Twelfth half-hour (15:00–15:30) alone gives R² 1.1%; r1 + r12 together give R² 2.6%. — [Gao et al. PDF](https://c.mql5.com/forextsd/forum/173/intraday_momentum_-_the_first_half-hour_return_predicts_the_last_half-hour_return.pdf)
- Out-of-sample R²: 1.2% (r1 alone), 0.7% (r12 alone), 1.8% (combined). — [Gao et al. PDF](https://c.mql5.com/forextsd/forum/173/intraday_momentum_-_the_first_half-hour_return_predicts_the_last_half-hour_return.pdf)
- Timing strategy (exact rule): at 15:30, go long SPY if r1 > 0, short if r1 < 0, exit at 16:00. Reported average return 6.67% p.a., st. dev. 6.19%, Sharpe 1.08, vs buy-and-hold 6.04% p.a. at 20.57% st. dev. The authors say outperformance remains significant after transaction costs. — [Gao et al. PDF](https://c.mql5.com/forextsd/forum/173/intraday_momentum_-_the_first_half-hour_return_predicts_the_last_half-hour_return.pdf)
- Regimes: predictability stronger on more volatile days (R² for combined predictors rises to 3.3% when first-half-hour volatility is high), higher-volume days, recession days, and major macro news days. Also found in 10 other actively traded ETFs and 2 international index futures. In a subsample (apparently the crisis period) slope rises to 13.6 with R² 4.1%, while r1 still predicts with R² 0.8% outside it, so much of the effect is concentrated in crisis periods. — [Gao et al. PDF](https://c.mql5.com/forextsd/forum/173/intraday_momentum_-_the_first_half-hour_return_predicts_the_last_half-hour_return.pdf)
- A later out-of-sample study reports that the predictability "disappears in the out-of-sample period" and that a Markov-switching model finds two regimes, with predictability depending on signal strength (venue and exact sample not confirmed). — via [search summary of ResearchGate "Understanding intraday momentum strategies"](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/363384228_Understanding_intraday_momentum_strategies) [WP/PR, not read in full]

**B. Baltussen, Da, Lammers & Martens (2021 JFE), "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum" [PR; not read directly]**
- Extends market intraday momentum to 60 futures markets, 1974–2020, and attributes it to dealers' short-gamma hedging (hedgers trade in the direction of the intraday move near the close). — [as summarized by FirmTape on dev.to](https://dev.to/firmtape/intraday-momentum-is-dead-in-the-0dte-era-we-measured-it-on-1085-spx-sessions-43g0)
- Mechanism has independent support elsewhere: a study on China ETFs finds intraday momentum is more significant on days of net (short) gamma positions. — [ScienceDirect, Delta-hedging demand and intraday momentum: China](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378437122003624) [PR]

**C. 0DTE-era test of Gao-style momentum [PRACT, vendor conflict]**
- 1,085 SPX sessions, 14 Apr 2022 to 20 Aug 2026; regression of last-30-min return on rest-of-day return (open to 15:30). Pooled slope +0.006 (t = +0.6); yearly slopes alternate sign (2022 +0.009, 2023 +0.002, 2024 −0.016, 2025 +0.016, 2026 −0.012; all |t| ≤ 1.0). Conclusion: unconditional effect is gone. Conditioning on the author's proprietary 0DTE dealer-gamma estimate, slope rises by +0.055 (t = +3.1) on short-gamma closes (about 15% of sessions); the public open-interest gamma convention shows no effect; author says this is not enough to trade. — [FirmTape, dev.to](https://dev.to/firmtape/intraday-momentum-is-dead-in-the-0dte-era-we-measured-it-on-1085-spx-sessions-43g0)
- Conflict: the author founded FirmTape, which sells SPX dealer-positioning data from the 0DTE tape. — [FirmTape, dev.to](https://dev.to/firmtape/intraday-momentum-is-dead-in-the-0dte-era-we-measured-it-on-1085-spx-sessions-43g0)

**D. Zarattini, Aziz & Barbon (2024), "Beat the Market: An Effective Intraday Momentum Strategy for S&P500 ETF (SPY)" [VENDOR-WP; SSRN 4824172; read full text]**
EXACT CODABLE RULES (from the paper):
- Data: SPY and VIX 1-minute OHLCV from IQFeed, May 2007 to April 2024. — [Paper PDF (U. St. Gallen repository)](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Step 1: for each of the last 14 days (i = 1..14) and each minute HH:MM, compute move_{t−i,HH:MM} = |Close_{t−i,HH:MM} / Open_{t−i,09:30} − 1|. Step 2: σ_{t,HH:MM} = average of those 14 moves (time-of-day specific). — [Paper PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Step 3 (gap-adjusted bands): UpperBound_{t,HH:MM} = max(Open_{t,09:30}, Close_{t−1,16:00}) × (1 + VM·σ_{t,HH:MM}); LowerBound = min(Open_{t,09:30}, Close_{t−1,16:00}) × (1 − VM·σ_{t,HH:MM}). Base case VM (volatility multiplier) = 1; best risk-adjusted VM in their sweep was about 1.5 (higher VM = fewer trades, lower total return). — [Paper PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Entry: check only at HH:00 and HH:30 (first possible entry 10:00). Long if price > UpperBound, short if price < LowerBound. — [Paper PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Exit: trailing stop evaluated only at HH:00/HH:30. Long stop = max(UpperBound_{t,HH:MM}, VWAP_{t,HH:MM}); short stop = min(LowerBound, VWAP) (VWAP from regular-hours data only). Crossing the opposite band reverses. All positions flat at 16:00 close. — [Paper PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Sizing: Shares_t = AUM_{t−1} × min(4, σ_target / σ_SPY,t) / Open_{t,09:30}, with σ_target = 2% daily and σ_SPY,t = 14-day st. dev. of daily SPY returns; leverage cap 4x. — [Paper PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Costs assumed: $0.0035/share commission (IBKR entry tier; about 20% of days at $0.0020 tier) plus $0.001/share slippage. Slippage was measured in a live experiment of over 1,000 random-direction $100k SPY market orders in April 2024 (mean slippage about $0.001, median $0.0005). — [Paper PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Results (in-sample 2007–Apr 2024): base (opposite-band stop, 100% exposure) 6.2% p.a., vol 10.9%, Sharpe 0.61; with current-band+VWAP stop 9.7% p.a., vol 7.7%, Sharpe 1.24, hit ratio 43%, MDD 12%; with volatility sizing 19.6% p.a., vol 14.3%, Sharpe 1.33, hit ratio 43%, MDD 25%, total 1,985%, beta −0.07. SPY buy-and-hold: 7.2% p.a., Sharpe 0.45, MDD 56%. 7,668 trades, about 1.8 trades/day, 37% trade-level hit ratio. Best trade +9.1%, worst −2.9%. — [Paper PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Regime results (in-sample): Sharpe rises with VIX at open (about 1.5 when VIX > 6, i.e. unconditional; about 3.5 when VIX > 40, then lower at extreme VIX because of few observations). Unconditional avg 12 bps/day (t = 5.34). NR4 days avg 22 bps (t 5.14, Sharpe 3.2); NR7 16 bps (t 3.07); Triangle 14 bps (t 3.19); days following a "Trend day" −2 bps (t −0.24); inside/outside days not significant. Day of week: Wed 18 bps (t 3.42, Sharpe 2.4) best, Mon 9 bps (t 1.84) weakest; authors link Wednesday strength to FOMC days. Gamma proxy: regressing daily strategy return on prior-day 5-day RSI of SPY gives β = −3.25 (p = 0.001), so momentum works better after recent declines (their proxy for dealers being short gamma). — [Paper PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Overfitting caveat in the paper itself: the VWAP stop and volatility sizing were added step by step and evaluated on the same 2007–2024 sample. — [Paper PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download); critics note the rules were calibrated on the data used to judge them — [Larry Swedroe Substack](https://larryswedroe.substack.com/p/when-beat-the-market-meets-the-real) [PRACT]

**Independent replications and post-publication decay of the noise-area strategy**
- Brusco replication [REPL]: SPY from July 2020 gives Sharpe 1.11 (vs paper 1.33), alpha about +16.7%/yr (t = 2.85), beta about 0; yearly Sharpe about 1.4–2.0 in each year 2020–2024, then **two years below zero** (2025–2026); recent Sharpe about zero on both SPY and ES. Trade stats: SPY about +2.6 bps/trade, 41% win, payoff ratio 1.69; **ES futures about +2 bps/trade, 36% win, payoff 2.1**, returns correlated 0.97 with SPY. ES costs modeled at about 0.4 bps per round trip; author rules out data feed, costs and contract roll as causes of the decline. 2022: +25.8% while SPY −19.5%. A 27-variant grid found the paper's original configuration best in-sample (no variant promoted); a claimed follow-up with Sharpe > 3 (Maróy 2025) was not supported; quarterly walk-forward reselection gave Sharpe 0.57 vs 0.92 fixed. Verdict: "Not allocable today, not dismissible as dead either." — [GitHub giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy)
- Paz Serpa OOS study (June 2026) [REPL/WP]: IQFeed 1-min SPY, exact rules, no parameter changes (14-day lookback, 30-min decision points, VWAP trail, 2% vol target, 4x cap). In-sample replication 2015–2024: 19.8% p.a., Sharpe 1.34 (matches paper). True OOS May 2024 to March 2026 "told a very different story" (exact numbers paywalled). — [Larry Swedroe Substack](https://larryswedroe.substack.com/p/when-beat-the-market-meets-the-real); [GitHub PazSheimy/spy-intraday-momentum-oos](https://github.com/PazSheimy/spy-intraday-momentum-oos)
- NQ futures rebuild (EdgeLab) [PRACT, sells strategies]: Databento 1-min NQ RTH 2010–2026, parameters fixed on 2010–2017, OOS 2018–2026, costs 0.01% round trip. Business-day Sharpe: long-only 0.90 IS / 0.68 OOS (about +88% cumulative, MDD about −11%, 6 of 9 OOS years positive); short-only −0.21 IS / 1.22 OOS (big years 2018 and 2022); both sides 0.43 IS / 1.39 OOS. Author concludes the long side is a real but modest edge and the short side's OOS gains are probably luck or vol-spike exposure; long-only in a "soft patch" since its 2024 peak. About 4,700 trades. — [EdgeLab Trading](https://edgelabtrading.com/blog/zarattini-intraday-momentum-nasdaq/)

**E. Crypto intraday momentum**
- Shen, Urquhart & Wang (2022), "Bitcoin intraday time series momentum," *Financial Review* 57(2), 319–344 [PR]. Because BTC trades 24/7, they use trading volume as a proxy for market trading time; the first half-hour positively predicts the last half-hour; predictability is greatest in the first sessions with the highest volume or volatility; attributed to liquidity provision; holds in and out of sample across exchanges (Bitstamp, Coinbase, Kraken); strategy gains largest in market downturns. — [Wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/fire.12290); [Birmingham repository](https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/en/publications/bitcoin-intraday-time-series-momentum/)
- Wen et al. (2022), "Intraday return predictability in the cryptocurrency markets: Momentum, reversal, or both" (*North American Journal of Economics and Finance*) [PR]: BTC 5-minute data 2013–2020; both the first and second-to-last half-hour returns predict the last half-hour, i.e. both intraday momentum and intraday reversal; patterns change around large jumps, FOMC announcements, liquidity levels and COVID-19. — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1062940822000833); [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/361202793_Intraday_return_predictability_in_the_cryptocurrency_markets_momentum_reversal_or_both) (author list and journal not verified from the page itself)
- Pre-registered independent OOS test, Binance USD-M perps BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT 5-min, Jan 2022 to Oct 2026, 1,764 trades per rule [REPL; pre-registration frozen before data download; merged 16 Nov 2026]:
  - R2 "Shen et al. on a fixed UTC clock": sign of 00:00–00:30 UTC return → hold the last half-hour. Gross −0.2 bp (BTC), 0.0 bp (ETH); net −10.2 bp at 10 bp taker round trip (t −13.5). **No gross edge.**
  - R1 "long 22:00–00:00 UTC" (from Padyšák & Vojtko, SSRN 4081000): gross +1.4 bp (t 0.84) BTC, +1.5 bp ETH; net −2.6 bp at 4 bp maker, −8.6 bp at 10 bp taker; negative every year 2022–2026; gross edge about one-sixth of what the 2015–21 source reported.
  - US-ETF benchmark window (15:00–16:00 ET): no drift (|t| ≤ 1.2); number of significant UTC-hour effects about what chance would produce.
  - Verdict: KILL. — [GitHub nishimweprince/trading-algos PR #98](https://github.com/nishimweprince/trading-algos/pull/98)
  - Caveat: this used a fixed 00:00 UTC "day", whereas Shen et al. define sessions by volume, so it is not an exact replication. — [search summary of Shen et al.](https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/en/publications/bitcoin-intraday-time-series-momentum/)

### Inferences
- Ranking of the evidence for intraday momentum: peer-reviewed in-sample evidence is strong (Gao; Baltussen), but every post-2022 out-of-sample test found here (FirmTape SPX 2022–26; Brusco SPY/ES 2025–26; Serpa SPY 2024–26) shows the unconditional effect weakened or gone. That fits the 0DTE story: dealers are often long gamma intraday, which dampens trends, and the effect survives, if at all, only on short-gamma days. The report should treat intraday momentum on ES/MES as "decayed, regime-dependent", not "proven".
- On ES/MES the noise-area edge was only about 2 bps/trade gross even in good years (Brusco). That is small relative to MES costs (see section 4), so the strategy works on ES/SPY with low per-bp costs but is fragile on micros.
- The best-supported crypto variant to code is Shen et al. with a volume-defined session or a high-volume / high-volatility day filter, but the only independent OOS test (fixed UTC clock) found no gross edge. Any crypto intraday momentum strategy should be treated as unproven.
- For a crypto port of the noise-area rule, there is no natural "open". A defensible choice would be 00:00 UTC (daily candle and funding reset) or 13:30 UTC (US equity open). Neither is tested in the sources found here.

### Gaps
- Exact OOS numbers of the Serpa (2026) study are paywalled.
- No peer-reviewed replication of the noise-area strategy found.
- Baltussen et al. (2021) was not read directly; their exact strategy rules, Sharpe ratios, and the companion "End-of-Day Reversal" paper by Baltussen, Da & Soebhag ([PDF](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/EOD.pdf)) were not checked. That paper appears directly relevant to post-2010s decay and should be checked.
- No published test of the noise-area rule on BTC/ETH perps was found.

## 2. Opening range breakout (Zarattini/Aziz, Crabel, Holmberg et al.) and crypto session opens

### Takeaway
The 5-minute ORB evidence comes almost entirely from vendor-affiliated SSRN papers (Zarattini, Aziz, Barbon). The QQQ version claims Sharpe 1.12 with zero slippage; the "stocks in play" version claims Sharpe 2.81 but only with a relative-volume screen of the top 20 stocks per day, and the unfiltered version earned Sharpe 0.48. An independent 2026 walk-forward test on MNQ (2021–2025) found **no ORB variant significant after costs**. No credible evidence was found for crypto session-open breakouts.

### Cited Findings

**A. Zarattini & Aziz (2023), "Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable? Evidence of sustainable long-term profits from ORB…" [VENDOR-WP; SSRN 4416622; read full text]**
EXACT CODABLE RULES (Table 1 of the paper):
- Instrument QQQ (or TQQQ). Opening range = first 5-minute candle (09:30–09:35 ET). If that candle closes up → long at the **open of the second 5-min candle** (09:35); if down → short; doji (open ≈ close) → no trade. Note: this is a direction-of-first-bar entry at 09:35, not a stop-order breakout of the high/low. — [Paper PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5983d931579fb366729580d8/t/643ed6765176b45506e41a01/1681839734183/SSRN-id4416622.pdf)
- Stop: low of first candle (long) / high of first candle (short); R = |entry − stop|. Target: 10R, otherwise exit at end of day. Size so a stop-out loses 1% of equity; max intraday leverage 4x; starting capital $25,000; commission $0.0005/share; **no slippage assumed**; sample 1 Jan 2016 to 17 Feb 2023. — [Paper PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5983d931579fb366729580d8/t/643ed6765176b45506e41a01/1681839734183/SSRN-id4416622.pdf)
- Results QQQ: 1,795 trades (51% long), total 675%, 31% p.a., Sharpe 1.12, annualized alpha 33% net of commissions, beta not significant; **win rate 24%, average 0.13R per trade**; losses capped near −1R. QQQ buy-and-hold 169% total. Leverage cap bound on about 10% of days. TQQQ version: total 1,484–1,485%, 46% p.a., alpha 47%, Sharpe 1.18. — [Paper PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5983d931579fb366729580d8/t/643ed6765176b45506e41a01/1681839734183/SSRN-id4416622.pdf)
- Parameter sweep (in-sample): best was stop = 5% of 14-day ATR with no target (exit EoD), giving 9,350% on TQQQ, but the authors themselves flag it as possibly unrealistic: the stop was about $0.08 on a $25 TQQQ and no slippage was modeled. — [Paper PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5983d931579fb366729580d8/t/643ed6765176b45506e41a01/1681839734183/SSRN-id4416622.pdf)

**B. Zarattini, Barbon & Aziz (2024), "A Profitable Day Trading Strategy For The U.S. Equity Market" (stocks-in-play ORB) [VENDOR-WP; SSRN 4729284]**
- Universe about 7,000 US stocks 2016–2023 (delisted included). Filters: price > $5, 14-day average volume ≥ 1M shares, 14-day ATR > $0.50. Relative volume = first-5-min volume / average first-5-min volume over the prior 14 days; require ≥ 100% and take the top 20 by relative volume each day. Entry: if first 5-min candle is up, buy-stop at its high; if down, sell-stop at its low; doji none. Stop = 10% of 14-day ATR. Exit at 16:00. Risk 1% per trade, 4x leverage cap. Costs: $0.0035/share commission; slippage reportedly not modeled. — [Tradoki summary](https://blog.tradoki.com/posts/opening-range-breakout-stocks-in-play-research) [PRACT]; [CXO Advisory](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/individual-investing/intraday-trading-of-overactive-stocks-via-opening-range-breakout/) [PRACT]; [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4729284)
- Results: top-20 stocks-in-play portfolio 1,637% total, 41.6% p.a., Sharpe 2.81, alpha 36%/yr; unfiltered 5-min ORB on all liquid stocks 29% total, 3.2% p.a., Sharpe 0.48, MDD 13%; S&P 500 198% total, Sharpe 0.78. Avg net trade −0.02R when relative volume < 100%, +0.08R above 100%, +0.38R above 3,000%. Opening-range length sensitivity with filter: 5-min best; 15-min 272%, 30-min 21%, 60-min 39% total. Win rates on the best stocks 17–24%. — [Tradoki](https://blog.tradoki.com/posts/opening-range-breakout-stocks-in-play-research); [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4729284)
- QuantConnect recreation [REPL]: Sharpe about 2.4 on a 1,000-stock universe vs 0.836 for SPY buy-and-hold. — [QuantConnect](https://www.quantconnect.com/research/18444/opening-range-breakout-for-stocks-in-play/); Tradoki says the QuantConnect reproduction covered a limited window and found no independent post-2023 test of the filtered version. — [Tradoki](https://blog.tradoki.com/posts/opening-range-breakout-stocks-in-play-research)
- Criticisms: commission only, no slippage, even though entry is a stop order in the volatile opening minutes; the edge depends on a relative-volume scanner; four range lengths tested (multiple-testing risk); one 8-year bull-dominated sample. — [Tradoki](https://blog.tradoki.com/posts/opening-range-breakout-stocks-in-play-research)

**C. Futures-specific ORB evidence**
- Independent single-author preprint (arXiv 2605.04004, 2026) [WP]: MNQ, 947 trading days 2021–2025, walk-forward, costs 2.0 index points ($4.00) round trip per micro. **No ORB variant passed significance tests**; the shortest holds lost money after costs; best was a long held 75 minutes at +2.82 points/trade, t = 0.88 vs required 2.0, unstable across years. — [as summarized by Tradoki](https://blog.tradoki.com/posts/opening-range-breakout-stocks-in-play-research) (arXiv paper itself not read)
- Holmberg, Lönnbark & Lundström (2013), "Assessing the profitability of intraday opening range breakout strategies," *Finance Research Letters* 10(1): 27–33, DOI 10.1016/j.frl.2012.09.001 [PR]. — [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v10y2013i1p27-33.html). Secondary description: ORB tested on US crude oil futures (and S&P 500 futures per one summary); ORB profitability rises with the underlying's volatility; "expansion" days differ from calmer contraction days. — [Lundström thesis references via search](https://swopec.hhs.se/umnees/abs/umnees0948.htm) (findings not verified from the primary text)
- Crabel's ORB (Toby Crabel, *Day Trading with Short Term Price Patterns and Opening Range Breakout*, 1990) is cited by Zarattini et al. as a predecessor volatility-breakout system, alongside Kaufman. — [Beat the Market PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download). No independent statistical test of Crabel's specific rules was found.

**D. Crypto session opens (Asia/London/NY, CME open, funding times)**
- The pre-registered Binance test found no drift in the US-ETF benchmark window (15:00–16:00 ET), no gross edge for the 00:00 UTC half-hour rule, and roughly chance-level counts of significant UTC-hour effects, 2022–2026. — [GitHub PR #98](https://github.com/nishimweprince/trading-algos/pull/98)
- No peer-reviewed study comparing Asian/London/New York session returns for BTC was found; the closest was an unvalidated Medium post claiming an ETH Asia-session reversal and a failed BTC Europe-to-US momentum strategy. — [Medium/Coinmonks](https://medium.com/coinmonks/crypto-markets-run-on-inverted-clocks-how-session-patterns-create-alpha-with-256-returns-e76a4c61e79c) [PRACT/MKT, "256% returns" headline]
- Minute BTC returns cluster at quarter-hour boundaries (+0.58 bps per minute concentrated at the turn of 15-min candles, negative in other minutes). — [arXiv 2402.11930, Stylized Facts of High-Frequency Bitcoin](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.11930) [WP / MDPI version]

### Inferences
- A codable MES/MNQ port of the QQQ ORB: at 09:35 ET take the direction of the 09:30–09:35 candle (skip doji, e.g. |close − open| < some fraction of ATR), stop at the opposite extreme of that candle, target 10R or flat at 15:59 (or 16:00 cash close / before 16:15 CME halt), risk 1% of equity per trade. Expect about 1 trade/day, about 24% win rate, average about +0.13R gross in the 2016–2023 sample. With a 0.13R expectancy, cost/R matters a lot: on MES with a typical first-bar range of several points, a $4 round trip on MNQ (2 pts) can eat a large share of edge. The MNQ study's null result is consistent with this.
- The strongest ORB result (stocks in play, Sharpe 2.81) is a **cross-sectional single-stock** effect driven by relative volume. It does **not** transfer to single-instrument index futures or BTC, where there is no "top 20" selection. For index futures, the evidence is the weaker QQQ version plus a null MNQ test.
- For crypto, the "open" is arbitrary. The only rigorous recent test found nothing at session boundaries. Session-open ORB on BTC/ETH has no credible evidence base.

### Gaps
- No post-2023 independent replication of the QQQ ORB was found (Concretum blog posts were not checked; vendor-run updates would carry a conflict anyway).
- Holmberg et al. (2013) primary results not verified (paywalled).
- No credible study of BTC returns around the CME futures open (17:00 CT Sunday/daily reopen) or around funding timestamps (00:00/08:00/16:00 UTC) was found in this pass.

## 3. Volatility-expansion breakouts (Bollinger/Keltner squeeze, Donchian, ATR filters) at minute horizons; short-horizon momentum vs reversal

### Takeaway
I found no credible, cost-adjusted, data-snooping-corrected evidence that Bollinger/Keltner squeeze or Donchian breakouts on 1–5-minute bars are profitable in ES/NQ or BTC. The literature on intraday technical rules finds that any gross predictability is wiped out by realistic costs. At minute horizons BTC shows negative autocorrelation (reversal), not momentum. The only "volatility" filters with supporting evidence work at the daily level (NR4/NR7 compression, VIX level) and are applied to the hours-long noise-area strategy, not to scalps.

### Cited Findings
- Neely & Weller (1999) intraday FX technical rules: strong out-of-sample predictability at zero cost, but excess returns "very sensitive" to transaction costs and liquidity; at the time only one other published paper examined intraday technical rules. — [Warwick WP 99-89 / search summary](https://text.www2.warwick.ac.uk/fac/soc/wbs/subjects/finance/research/wpaperseries/1999/99-89.pdf) [PR/WP]
- Sullivan, Timmermann & White (data-snooping reality check): best technical rule from 1897–1986 shows no significant superior performance in 1987–1996 or on S&P 500 futures. — [SSRN id1095202 / search summary](https://smallake.kr/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/SSRN-id1095202.pdf) [PR]
- Bajgrowicz & Scaillet: even in-sample, technical-rule performance is completely offset by low transaction costs. — [Bajgrowicz & Scaillet PDF](https://bashtage.github.io/kevinsheppard.com/files/teaching/mfe/advanced-econometrics/Bajgrowicz_Scaillet.pdf) [PR, JFE 2012]
- A recent multi-market study finds technical-rule results very sensitive to moderate costs. — [Springer, Financial Markets and Portfolio Management 2023](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11408-023-00433-2.pdf) [PR; OOS conclusion not read]
- BTC minute horizons: negative return autocorrelation at 1-, 5- and 15-minute horizons, linked partly to liquidation cascades. — [Ledger journal, "On the Intraday Behavior of Bitcoin"](https://ledgerjournal.org/ojs/ledger/article/download/213/212/1232) [PR]. Microstructure (bid-ask bounce) can create spurious negative autocorrelation in trade prices; use mid-quotes to check. — [same source / search summary](https://ledgerjournal.org/ojs/ledger/article/download/213/212/1232)
- Daily-level compression filters that do help (in-sample, vendor paper): NR4 and NR7 days raise noise-area momentum returns to 22 and 16 bps/day vs 12 unconditional. — [Beat the Market PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Noise-area bands are themselves a time-of-day-adjusted volatility breakout. The authors say the time-varying band is what distinguishes the strategy from the Crabel/Kaufman volatility breakouts. — [Beat the Market PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Peer-reviewed global intraday TSMOM exists: predictability holds in 12 of 16 markets. — [ScienceDirect, "Intraday time series momentum: Global evidence and links to market characteristics"](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S138641812100001X) [PR, J. Financial Markets 2021; details not read]

### Inferences
- A volatility-expansion breakout scalper on 1–5-minute bars has no evidence base. It also trades against the documented minute-level reversal in BTC. It should rank last unless the bot's own walk-forward test shows otherwise.
- If a volatility-expansion idea is used, the evidence supports (a) a time-of-day-normalized band (noise area) rather than a fixed Bollinger/Donchian band, since intraday volatility is U-shaped, and (b) holding to the close rather than taking small scalp targets.

### Gaps
- No study testing Bollinger/Keltner squeeze or Donchian breakouts on 1–5-minute ES/NQ or BTC data with costs and snooping correction was found. This is an absence of evidence, not proven failure.
- No intraday TSMOM paper specific to CME index futures at minute horizons was read in full.

## 4. Costs and net-of-cost viability (trade frequency, win rate, break-even)

### Takeaway
These are low-win-rate, positive-skew strategies (ORB win rate about 24% at +0.13R; noise-area trade win rate 36–43% with payoff 1.7–2.1) whose edge per trade is small: about 2–2.6 bps per trade on SPY/ES in the replications. They are viable on ES/SPY with cost about 0.4 bps per round trip, marginal on MES/MNQ, and not viable at Binance VIP0 taker fees (10 bps round trip) unless gross edge per trade is several times larger than any measured.

### Cited Findings
- Noise area: about 1.8 trades/day, 37% trade hit rate, 43% daily hit rate (paper). — [Beat the Market PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download). Replication: SPY +2.6 bps/trade (41% win, payoff 1.69); ES +2 bps/trade (36% win, payoff 2.1), ES costs about 0.4 bps per round trip. — [Brusco GitHub](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy)
- The paper's cost assumption ($0.0035 commission + $0.001 slippage per share, about 0.1 bp at SPY around $500) is low; the authors show total return falls as commission rises and tell traders to evaluate their own costs. — [Beat the Market PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- QQQ ORB: about 1 trade/day (1,795 trades over about 7.1 years), win rate 24%, average +0.13R; commission $0.0005/share and zero slippage. — [ORB PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5983d931579fb366729580d8/t/643ed6765176b45506e41a01/1681839734183/SSRN-id4416622.pdf)
- MNQ ORB: $4.00 (2 pts) round-trip cost assumption; no variant significant net. — [Tradoki summary of arXiv 2605.04004](https://blog.tradoki.com/posts/opening-range-breakout-stocks-in-play-research)
- Binance USD-M perps VIP0: 0.05% taker per side (10 bps round trip); optimistic maker 4 bps round trip. Rules with +1.4 bp gross went to −2.6 bp (maker) / −8.6 bp (taker). — [GitHub PR #98](https://github.com/nishimweprince/trading-algos/pull/98)
- NQ noise-area rebuild used 0.01% (1 bp) round trip and warns live results will be lower, especially on 1-minute data. — [EdgeLab](https://edgelabtrading.com/blog/zarattini-intraday-momentum-nasdaq/)

### Inferences
- Break-even arithmetic (my calculation; contract specs are standard CME specs, not sourced here): MES = $5/index point, tick 0.25 = $1.25. At ES about 6,000, 1 MES ≈ $30,000 notional, so 1 bp ≈ $3. A typical all-in MES round trip (commission + exchange/NFA fees about $1–2, plus 1 tick of slippage per side = $2.50) is about $3.5–4.5, i.e. **about 1.2–1.5 bps**. That is **3–4x the 0.4 bps the ES replication assumed**, and at or below the 2 bps/trade gross edge of noise-area momentum on ES. Net edge on MES is therefore roughly zero to slightly positive, even in years when the strategy worked. On full ES, commissions per notional are about 10x smaller and slippage per tick is the same in index points, so ES (≈ $300k notional) is materially better if capital allows.
- BTC perps: 10 bps round-trip taker cost is about 5x the gross per-trade edge measured for noise-area momentum on ES. BTC daily vol is roughly 3–4x ES (assumption), so a mechanically scaled edge might be about 6–10 bps/trade. That is near break-even at taker fees, and would need maker-only entries (post-only limits) to have a chance. The only OOS crypto test of intraday-timing rules found negative net returns even at maker fees.
- ORB on futures: expectancy is quoted in R, so cost as a fraction of R is the key metric. With R = first-5-min range (often 5–15 MES points ≈ $25–75 per contract) and costs about $4, cost is about 5–15% of R, consuming roughly 40–100% of a 0.13R gross expectancy. This explains why the MNQ study found nothing.
- Sensitivity: both vendor papers assume costs at or near the cheapest retail tier, and the ORB paper assumes zero slippage. Published Sharpe ratios should be haircut heavily for retail futures/crypto implementation.

### Gaps
- No sourced, current (2026) all-in MES/MNQ commission schedule was retrieved. The ~$1–2 broker+exchange figure above is an unsourced estimate to verify with the chosen broker (e.g., IBKR, NinjaTrader, Tradovate).
- No measured slippage study for MES/MNQ stop orders at 09:35 ET or for BTC perp market orders was found.

## 5. Regime filters (volatility, trend vs range days, day-of-week, news days, relative volume)

### Takeaway
The best-documented filters are: higher volatility (VIX / first-half-hour realized vol) improves intraday momentum; daily compression (NR4/NR7) improves noise-area returns; macro news / FOMC days strengthen momentum; recent market declines (proxy for dealer short gamma) help; and for single-stock ORB, relative volume is the whole edge. All of these come from in-sample analyses, mostly vendor papers, and some (day-of-week) are clear data-mining risks.

### Cited Findings
- Gao et al.: stronger on high-volatility, high-volume, recession, and major macro-news days. — [Gao et al. PDF](https://c.mql5.com/forextsd/forum/173/intraday_momentum_-_the_first_half-hour_return_predicts_the_last_half-hour_return.pdf)
- Noise area: Sharpe rises with VIX at open (about 3.5 for VIX > 40); NR4/NR7/Triangle days better; post-trend-day not significant; Wednesday best (linked to FOMC), Monday weakest; low prior 5-day RSI (recent declines) → higher returns (β = −3.25, p = 0.001). — [Beat the Market PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Noise-area in practice: best years were crisis/bear years (e.g. 2022 +25.8% while SPY −19.5%); NQ short side earned mostly in 2018 and 2022. — [Brusco GitHub](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy); [EdgeLab](https://edgelabtrading.com/blog/zarattini-intraday-momentum-nasdaq/)
- Dealer gamma: short-gamma closes (about 15% of sessions) show continuation in the 0DTE era (t = 3.1) using proprietary gamma data; public OI-based gamma shows nothing. — [FirmTape, dev.to](https://dev.to/firmtape/intraday-momentum-is-dead-in-the-0dte-era-we-measured-it-on-1085-spx-sessions-43g0)
- Stocks-in-play ORB: edge appears only with relative volume ≥ 100% (−0.02R below, +0.08R above, +0.38R above 3,000%). — [Tradoki](https://blog.tradoki.com/posts/opening-range-breakout-stocks-in-play-research)
- BTC: Shen et al. predictability greatest on highest-volume/volatility sessions; Wen et al. patterns change around jumps and FOMC. — [Birmingham repository](https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/en/publications/bitcoin-intraday-time-series-momentum/); [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1062940822000833)
- Holmberg et al.: ORB profitability rises with volatility of the underlying (secondary summary). — [IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v10y2013i1p27-33.html)

### Inferences
- A defensible, low-parameter regime overlay for MES/ES noise-area momentum: trade only when VIX at open ≥ some threshold (e.g. ≥ 20), or scale size by VIX/realized vol; optionally size up on NR4/NR7 days and on days after a 5-day decline (low RSI5). Do not use day-of-week filters (multiple-testing risk). These thresholds were not individually validated OOS in any source found.
- A relative-volume analogue for index futures (first-5-min volume vs its 14-day average) is plausible but untested in the sources.

### Gaps
- No OOS validation of the VIX or NR4/NR7 overlays was found.
- No source tested news-day (CPI/FOMC) handling for ORB on futures.

## 6. Failure modes and decay (publication decay, crowding, 0DTE, overfitting)

### Takeaway
All three strategy families show the classic pattern: strong in the original sample, weaker or gone after publication and in the 0DTE era (2022+). Noise-area momentum Sharpe fell to about 0 in 2025–2026 on SPY and ES. Unconditional Gao momentum is insignificant in SPX 2022–2026. The MNQ ORB is null 2021–2025. Crypto session/half-hour rules are dead 2022–2026. Vendor conflicts and step-by-step in-sample rule additions raise overfitting risk.

### Cited Findings
- Noise area: Sharpe 1.4–2.0 each year 2020–2024, then two years below zero (2025–2026); recent Sharpe about 0 on SPY and ES; walk-forward re-optimization worse than fixed (0.57 vs 0.92). — [Brusco GitHub](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy)
- Serpa: OOS May 2024 to Mar 2026 results differ sharply from the in-sample Sharpe of 1.34. — [Swedroe Substack](https://larryswedroe.substack.com/p/when-beat-the-market-meets-the-real)
- Gao-style momentum in the 0DTE era: pooled t = 0.6, 2022–2026. — [FirmTape, dev.to](https://dev.to/firmtape/intraday-momentum-is-dead-in-the-0dte-era-we-measured-it-on-1085-spx-sessions-43g0)
- Earlier OOS work: Gao predictability disappears out of sample; regime-dependent. — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/363384228_Understanding_intraday_momentum_strategies)
- Crypto R1 rule's gross edge about one-sixth of its 2015–21 source; R2 no gross edge 2022–2026. — [GitHub PR #98](https://github.com/nishimweprince/trading-algos/pull/98)
- The noise-area paper itself notes that 0DTE options spread option-expiration effects across all weekdays. — [Beat the Market PDF](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/download)
- Overfitting: ORB paper's best variant (5% ATR stop, EoD exit) was chosen from a stop × target heatmap on the same sample; four OR lengths tested in the stocks-in-play paper. — [ORB PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5983d931579fb366729580d8/t/643ed6765176b45506e41a01/1681839734183/SSRN-id4416622.pdf); [Tradoki](https://blog.tradoki.com/posts/opening-range-breakout-stocks-in-play-research)
- Conflicts of interest: Zarattini (Concretum Research/Group) and Aziz (Peak Capital Trading, Bear Bull Traders) are commercial; the ORB paper lists contacts at peakcapitaltrading.com and concretumresearch.com. — [ORB PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5983d931579fb366729580d8/t/643ed6765176b45506e41a01/1681839734183/SSRN-id4416622.pdf); [Concretum Group](https://concretumgroup.com/a-profitable-day-trading-strategy-for-the-u-s-equity-market/). EdgeLab and FirmTape also sell products. — [EdgeLab](https://edgelabtrading.com/blog/zarattini-intraday-momentum-nasdaq/); [FirmTape](https://dev.to/firmtape/intraday-momentum-is-dead-in-the-0dte-era-we-measured-it-on-1085-spx-sessions-43g0)

### Inferences
- McLean & Pontiff (2016, JF) found that anomaly returns fall about 26% out of sample and about 58% post-publication (from general knowledge; not retrieved in this pass, so verify before citing). The observed decay here (Sharpe from about 1.3 to about 0 for noise area within about 1.5 years of the May 2024 SSRN posting) is far larger than that average. That suggests regime change (0DTE / dealer long gamma, low-vol trending 2025 tape) and/or crowding, on top of in-sample overfitting.
- Because the strategy historically earned most in crisis/high-VIX years, a near-zero Sharpe in calm years may be consistent with its design: it behaves like a long-volatility / crisis-alpha sleeve rather than a steady earner. The report should present it that way, not as a scalping income strategy.
- Ranking suggestion for a retail bot (my judgement from the above): (1) noise-area intraday momentum on ES (or MES with care), long-biased or VIX-gated, holding hours to close, as the best-documented but currently decayed option; (2) 5-minute ORB on MES/MNQ, weak and with a null independent test; (3) crypto intraday momentum / session rules, no OOS support; (4) 1–5-minute volatility-expansion scalps, no evidence and contrary to minute-level reversal. None clears a high bar for live capital without the bot's own walk-forward and paper-trading validation.

### Gaps
- No primary-source quantification of ORB crowding was found.
- McLean & Pontiff (2016) figures not retrieved in this session (verify).
- No source isolated whether 2025–2026 decay is regime (low vol) or structural (0DTE). Brusco and Serpa document the decay but not its cause.
